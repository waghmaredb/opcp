**Open Process Control Plane (OPCP)** is an open framework that lets a business keep its process logic, context and authority, while renting capabilities from any supplier. This is a draft for comment.

## Try it without installing anything

Download **opcp-exit-readiness-form-0.2.0.html**, open it in any browser, and answer the questions for one critical process. It works offline; nothing you type leaves your device. To see what the resulting report looks like, open **opcp-example-report-0.2.0.html** (a hypothetical bank).

To turn your answers into a report, install the package (Python 3.10+):

```bash
pip install opcp-0.2.0-py3-none-any.whl
opcp scorecard run answers.json -o report.html
```

## What's in 0.2.0

- **Exit-Readiness Scorecard.** It rates how dependent each critical process is on its supplier: logic ownership, context, authority, lock-in, contract exit terms, time-to-swap, fallback, and concentration across processes.
- **Standard question bank.** 22 questions with stable IDs, anchored options, a named respondent role for each, triage and consistency checks.
- **Evidence levels.** Every answer is Declared, Documented or Tested, and every measure shows its weakest evidence.
- **74 clause-level references** to 15 regulations, guidelines and standards: DORA and its RTS, the EU Data Act, EBA, APRA CPS 230, MAS, PRA, FSB, the Basel Committee, IOSCO, NIST and CSA.
- **20 verified research sources** explaining why each measure matters (`docs/EVIDENCE.md`).
- **Specification** for capability archetypes, the standard envelope, two-stage routing and an authority gate based on Reversibility Classes R0-R4.
- **Reference runtime** with adapter and runtime conformance checks.
- **DORA register export.** `opcp scorecard export answers.json -o dora.csv` suggests EU DORA register of information values from your answers, with the reasoning for each. These are suggestions to review, not a filing.
- **Pilot playbook and swap-drill guide.** How to run the scorecard across three processes in six weeks, and how to rehearse a supplier swap safely.

## Status and limits

- **Self-assessment, not certification.** Reports are a self-assessment against a published method, not a certification, and references show relevance, not compliance.
- **Check locators first.** Confirm every locator against the official text before relying on it; `docs/REFERENCES.md` records how each source was checked.
- **Expect change.** The specification and question bank will change based on feedback.
